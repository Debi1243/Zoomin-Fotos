About

A small studio that likes people more than poses.

Zoomin Fotos is a photography studio based in Bhubaneswar. We photograph weddings, families, milestones and the businesses that make Odisha what it is.

Our story

We started Zoomin Fotos because the photographs people treasure most are rarely the perfect ones. They are the ones where everyone looks like themselves.

So we work quietly, direct gently and spend our time noticing: the aunt fixing a dupatta, the groom's father pretending not to cry, the toddler who refuses to stand still. Those are the frames that end up on walls.

Every gallery is edited by hand, in colours that match what you saw on the day, and kept safely backed up long after delivery. When it is time for an album or a print, we design and produce it ourselves.

Our approach

We would rather catch the laugh after the pose.

  1. 01

    Direction, not posing

    We give small prompts and then step back. The best expressions happen between instructions.

  2. 02

    Honest colour

    Skin tones stay true and reds stay red. We edit for how the day felt, not for the latest filter.

  3. 03

    Edited by hand

    No presets on autopilot. Each delivered photograph is looked at, corrected and finished individually.

  4. 04

    Backed up, always

    Dual-card cameras and same-night backups in two places. Your photographs are never on a single card.
