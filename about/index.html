A photography studio in Bhubaneswar that likes people more than poses

Zoomin Fotos photographs weddings, families, milestones and the businesses that make Odisha what it is, with prices you can see before you call.

Our story

The photos people treasure most are the ones where everyone looks like themselves.

So we work quietly, direct gently and spend our time noticing: the aunt fixing a dupatta, the groom's father pretending not to cry, the toddler who refuses to stand still. Those are the frames that end up on walls.

We also think booking a photographer should be simple. That is why our packages and prices are on the website, and why you can ask us anything on WhatsApp.
