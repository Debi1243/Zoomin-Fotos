FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

What is included in a package?

Each package lists its team, photos, videos and album on the packages page. Every wedding package includes all raw photos and videos, a full-length edited video and a printed album; the bigger packages add edited photos, highlight films, reels and a drone.

Is GST included in the price?

No. All prices are shown before GST, which is added to your final invoice.

How do I confirm my booking?

Send your date through the quick form or on WhatsApp. We check availability and agree the details with you. Your date is booked once we have both confirmed and the advance is paid.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. Custom packages available based on your event requirements. You can also build your own wedding package event by event in the package builder, adding photographers, cinematographers, a drone, an album or a same-day edit, and see an itemised estimate before you ask.

Do you travel outside Bhubaneswar?

Yes. We photograph across Odisha and travel further for weddings and shoots. Travel and stay are quoted separately, so you see them before you book.

When will we receive our photographs?

It depends on the package and the size of the shoot. We tell you the delivery date in your quote, before you book, so you know exactly when to expect your photos and videos.

What happens if my event date changes?

Tell us as soon as you know. We will check the new date and confirm what changes, if anything, before you commit. Date-change terms are written into your quote.

What are the cancellation terms?

Cancellation terms are set out in writing in your quote, before you pay any advance. Ask us on WhatsApp if you would like to see them first.

Your story deserves to be told beautifully.

Send us your date and venue. We'll tell you straight away if we're free, and help you choose the right package.
