FAQ

Questions about ordering

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Can I order prints of photos that Zoomin Fotos didn't take?

Yes. Send us any photo in good resolution and we will check it, correct the colour and tell you the largest size it will print well at.

How long does an order take?

Prints, frames and gifts usually take a few working days; albums take longer because you approve the design first. We give you the date when we confirm your order.

How do I pay?

Nothing is charged on this website. Once we confirm your order and the final price on WhatsApp, we send you the payment details.

Do you deliver outside Bhubaneswar?

Ask us on WhatsApp. Delivery depends on the size of the order and the city, so we add it to your quote before you pay.

Haven't had your photoshoot yet?

Every wedding package already includes a printed album. Book your shoot and we'll make the rest of the prints for you too.
