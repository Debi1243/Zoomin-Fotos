FAQ

Questions about packages

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

What is included in a package?

Each package lists its team, photos, videos and album on the packages page. Every wedding package includes all raw photos and videos, a full-length edited video and a printed album; the bigger packages add edited photos, highlight films, reels and a drone.

Is GST included in the price?

No. All prices are shown before GST, which is added to your final invoice.

How do I confirm my booking?

Send your date through the quick form or on WhatsApp. We check availability and agree the details with you. Your date is booked once we have both confirmed and the advance is paid.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. Every price is negotiable, and you can build your own wedding package event by event, adding photographers, cinematographers, a drone, an album or a same-day edit.
