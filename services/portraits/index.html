How we work

Simple to book. Calm on the day.

Four steps from first message to finished gallery, with a real person answering at every one.

  1. Step 01

    Enquire

    Tell us your date, place and what you have in mind. We reply within a day with availability and a tailored quote.

    • Date check
    • Tailored quote
    • No obligation

  2. Step 02

    Plan

    A call to understand the people, the schedule and the moments that matter most, so we are always in the right place.

    • Planning call
    • Shot list
    • Timeline

  3. Step 03

    Shoot

    We work quietly and direct gently. You enjoy the day; we make sure it is remembered properly.

    • Candid coverage
    • Gentle direction
    • Backup cameras

  4. Step 04

    Deliver

    A preview within 72 hours, then a private online gallery of individually edited images, plus albums and prints if you wish.

    • 72-hour preview
    • Private gallery
    • Albums & prints

FAQ

Portraits, answered.

Can we bring our pets?

Please do. Tell us in advance so we can plan the session around them.

How many people can be in a family session?

Up to twelve people in a standard session. Larger gatherings are quoted as an event.

How long until we receive our photographs?

You get a hand-picked preview within 72 hours. The full, individually edited gallery follows within three to four weeks, depending on the size of the shoot.
