FAQ

Commercial: common questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Do you provide props and styling?

Basic surfaces and props are included. Food and prop stylists can be arranged for larger shoots.

Can you match our brand guidelines?

Yes. Share your guidelines and any reference images, and we will match colour and mood across every shot.

When will we receive our photographs?

It depends on the package and the size of the shoot. We tell you the delivery date in your quote, before you book, so you know exactly when to expect your photos and videos.

Is GST included in the price?

No. All prices are shown before GST, which is added to your final invoice.

How do I confirm my booking?

Send your date through the quick form or on WhatsApp. We check availability and agree the details with you. Your date is booked once we have both confirmed and the advance is paid.

What happens if my event date changes?

Tell us as soon as you know. We will check the new date and confirm what changes, if anything, before you commit. Date-change terms are written into your quote.

What are the cancellation terms?

Cancellation terms are set out in writing in your quote, before you pay any advance. Ask us on WhatsApp if you would like to see them first.
