FAQ

Wedding cinematography: common questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Is a cinematographer included in every wedding package?

Yes. Essence and Signature include one cinematographer; Luxe and Infinity include two. Every package includes all raw videos and a full-length edited video.

How long is the highlight film?

Signature and Luxe include a 4 to 5 minute highlight video. Infinity includes a 7 to 10 minute cinematic, storyline-driven highlight film.

Can you live stream our wedding?

Yes. Live streaming is ₹8,000 per event and can be added in the package builder.

Do you offer drone shots?

Infinity includes a drone. With any other package, a drone is ₹5,000 per event. Some venues need permission for drones, which we ask you to arrange.

Is GST included in the price?

No. All prices are shown before GST, which is added to your final invoice.

How do I confirm my booking?

Send your date through the quick form or on WhatsApp. We check availability and agree the details with you. Your date is booked once we have both confirmed and the advance is paid.

What happens if my event date changes?

Tell us as soon as you know. We will check the new date and confirm what changes, if anything, before you commit. Date-change terms are written into your quote.

What are the cancellation terms?

Cancellation terms are set out in writing in your quote, before you pay any advance. Ask us on WhatsApp if you would like to see them first.

Your story deserves to be told beautifully.

Send us your date and venue. We'll tell you straight away if we're free, and help you choose the right package.
