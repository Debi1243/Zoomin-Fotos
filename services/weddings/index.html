FAQ

Weddings: common questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Are the package prices fixed?

Prices are for one day of coverage, plus GST, and are negotiable. Multi-day weddings, travel and add-ons are quoted together so you get one clear number.

How far in advance should we book?

As soon as you know your date. Dates are booked in the order they are confirmed, so send yours early and we will tell you straight away if it is free.

When will we receive our photographs?

It depends on the package and the size of the shoot. We tell you the delivery date in your quote, before you book, so you know exactly when to expect your photos and videos.

Do you travel outside Bhubaneswar?

Yes. We photograph across Odisha and travel further for weddings and shoots. Travel and stay are quoted separately, so you see them before you book.
