FAQ

Maternity & Newborn: common questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

When should we book a newborn session?

During pregnancy. We pencil in your due date and confirm the session once the baby arrives.

Is the studio safe for newborns?

Yes. It is kept warm, quiet and clean, and we never use poses that rely on balance or props to hold the baby.

When will we receive our photographs?

It depends on the package and the size of the shoot. We tell you the delivery date in your quote, before you book, so you know exactly when to expect your photos and videos.
