# Zoomin Fotos website audit, 23 Oct 2026

Local copy: `D:\Zoomin Fotos\Website`, branch `main`, up to date with `origin/main` (a65787f).
Nothing has been committed or pushed.

## 1. Architecture

- Next.js 16.3 (App Router, Turbopack), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, Motion, Lenis, Zod. npm.
- Live site: https://zoominfotos.in, a Docker container (`output: standalone`) on the studio server behind a Cloudflare Tunnel.
  `deploy/auto-deploy.sh` pulls `main` every minute and rebuilds. **Any push to `main` goes live in about 3 minutes.**
- There is also a GitHub Pages workflow (static export) for the `gh-pages` branch.
- Content is in JSON files (`src/content/*.json`) and `src/lib/data.ts`. The `/admin` pages edit those files by committing to GitHub.
- Pages: `/`, `/about`, `/portfolio`, `/services`, `/services/[6 slugs]`, `/contact`, `/testimonials`, `/portal` (client portal), `/admin/*` (noindex).
- Integrations:
  - Contact form: a Server Action that POSTs to `CONTACT_WEBHOOK_URL` (set on the server).
  - Optional Google Sheet endpoint for analytics, enquiries and reviews (`settings.json` → `dataEndpoint`). Currently empty.
  - Cloudflare Web Analytics beacon and email obfuscation, injected by Cloudflare.
  - YouTube films (`videos.json`, currently empty).
- Baseline checks before my changes: lint passed (1 warning, in the OG image), typecheck passed and production build passed.
  All 26 routes prerender as static.

## 2. Confirmed findings

### Trust and content

- Reviews: `testimonials.json` is empty. The site already handles this honestly: no fake reviews and no rating schema.
  It links to the Google listing and invites reviews. No change needed.
- Portfolio: 68 real photos across all six services. Placeholder frames exist in code but are never shown,
  because every service has real photos. No missing image files.
- **The delivery copy contradicts the packages.**
  - The FAQ and process copy promise a "full, individually edited gallery within three to four weeks" and say
    "each delivered photograph is edited individually".
  - But the Essence package has no edited photos (raw files, album and film only), and Signature has 20 edited photos.
- The weddings intro says "a lead photographer and a second shooter", but Essence has one photographer.
- Pre-wedding: the "You receive" list says "Two outfit changes", but the FAQ says "one outfit change".
- Claims with no evidence in the repo that need your confirmation:
  - "72-hour preview"
  - "Dual-card cameras and same-night backups in two places"
  - "reply within one working day"
  - "dates fill six to nine months ahead"
  - "hold the date for a week"
  - "travel at cost, no markup"
  - "usage licence" (commercial)
  - "same-day selects" (events)
- About page: there is no photographer or team introduction. There are no names, photos or experience details in the repo.

### Conversion

- There was no WhatsApp link anywhere, although the phone number was labelled "Phone or WhatsApp". **Fixed.**
- The package "Enquire" buttons opened a blank form and dropped the chosen package. **Fixed.**
- The home page had no price guidance; prices were only on the service pages and in the FAQ text. **Fixed.**
- The form is solid:
  - It validates on the client and the server, focuses the first error and has a honeypot.
  - The button is disabled while sending, which prevents double submits.
  - It reports errors honestly and never shows success when sending fails in production.
- On phones, `/contact#enquiry` lands on the contact list, and the form starts below it. Not changed.

### Performance (Lighthouse 12 lab data. This is not field data, and there is no CrUX data yet)

- Live home page, mobile:
  - Score 52
  - LCP 5.0 s
  - FCP 2.2 s
  - TBT 1,400 ms
  - CLS 0
  - 963 KiB, 1,703 DOM elements
- Live home page, desktop: score 94, LCP 1.2 s, TBT 90 ms, CLS 0.
- The mobile LCP element was the hero headline, and 93% of its LCP was render delay. The cause was a page-wide
  fade-in plus a word-by-word headline animation, both waiting on JavaScript.
- Heavy client JS: Motion, Lenis smooth scroll, an always-running photo marquee and a scroll progress spring.
  About 128 KiB is unused on the home page.
- Images use `next/image` with `sizes`, AVIF/WebP and fixed aspect ratios (CLS 0). The photos are already WebP,
  12 MB in total.

### SEO

- Good: titles, descriptions, canonicals, `sitemap.xml`, OG image, Service, Breadcrumb and FAQ JSON-LD,
  `/admin` and `/portal` noindex.
- `robots.txt` has a Lighthouse "invalid" error. It comes from the Cloudflare-managed `Content-Signal` block, which is a
  Cloudflare dashboard setting, not code. Google ignores unknown lines.
- Accessibility: 12 featured-photo buttons had names that didn't match their visible text. **Fixed.**
